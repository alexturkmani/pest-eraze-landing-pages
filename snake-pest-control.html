Why Act Now

Snakes Are Dangerous — Don't Take Chances

Melbourne is home to some of the world's most venomous snakes. If you've spotted one on your property, keep your distance and call a professional immediately.

🐍

Venomous Species

Melbourne's suburbs are home to tiger snakes, eastern brown snakes, lowland copperheads, and red-bellied black snakes — all of which can deliver potentially fatal bites.

⚠ Life-threatening bites possible
👦

Risk to Families & Pets

Children and pets are at the highest risk from snake encounters. Pets often disturb snakes sheltering in gardens, sheds, and under decking, leading to bites.

⚠ Pets & kids most vulnerable
☀️

Seasonal Activity

Snakes are most active from October to April as they come out to bask, feed, and mate. Warm days bring snakes into yards, garages, and even inside homes.

⚠ Peak activity Oct–April

Warning Signs

Signs Snakes Are on Your Property

!

Snake Sighting

The most obvious sign — you've seen a snake in your garden, garage, shed, or even inside the house. Keep your distance and call us immediately.

!

Shed Skins

Finding snake skins in your garden, shed, or under the house indicates a snake has been sheltering nearby. Snakes shed their skin several times a year.

!

Slither Trails

S-shaped trails in dusty areas, soil, or mulch indicate snake movement. Check around water features, compost bins, and along fence lines.

!

Rodent or Frog Activity

If you have mice, rats, or lots of frogs around your property, you have a food source that attracts snakes. Address the prey, reduce the predator.
