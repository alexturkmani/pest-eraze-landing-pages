Is This For You?

Who Needs a Termite Inspection?

Buying a Home

Pre-purchase inspections save you from inheriting hidden termite damage. Essential due diligence.

Annual Check-Up

Australian Standard AS 3660 recommends annual termite inspections for all properties. Stay protected year-round.

Spotted Warning Signs

Mud tubes, hollow-sounding timber, sagging floors, tight-fitting doors — all signs you need an inspection now.

Before Renovating

Renovations can disturb termite colonies and spread them. Always inspect before opening up walls.
