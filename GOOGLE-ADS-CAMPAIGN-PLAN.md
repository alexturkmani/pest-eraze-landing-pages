## BIDDING STRATEGY — How to Get Position 1

### Phase 1 (First 2 weeks): Learning
- **Strategy:** Maximize Clicks
- **Why:** Google needs click data before you can bid for position. This collects data fast.
- **Daily budget:** Start with the lower end of each campaign's range

### Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4): Positioning
- **Strategy:** Target Impression Share
- **Target:** Top of page, 90-95%
- **Max CPC bid limit:** Set at $8-12 for general pest, $10-15 for termites, $6-10 for others
- **Why:** This tells Google "I want to be at the top of the page for X% of eligible auctions"

### Phase 3 (Month 2+): Conversion Optimized
- **Strategy:** Target CPA or Maximize Conversions
- **Requires:** At least 15-30 conversions per campaign
- **Why:** Once Google knows what a conversion looks like, let it optimize for cost per lead

### Key Settings:
- **Networks:** Search ONLY (turn OFF Display Network, turn OFF Search Partners)
- **Location:** Melbourne metro area (or specific suburbs you service)
- **Location options:** "People IN or regularly in your targeted locations" (NOT "interested in")
- **Schedule:** All day, or adjust bid +20% during peak hours (7am-9am, 5pm-8pm)
- **Device:** All devices, but monitor and adjust. Mobile typically converts higher for emergency services (snakes, wasps)
