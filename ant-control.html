Why Treatment Matters

Killing the Ones You See Won't Fix It

The ants on your kitchen bench are just scouts. The colony — often 100,000+ strong — is hidden in walls, under slabs or in the garden.

🐜

Massive Colonies

A single ant colony can contain hundreds of thousands of workers. Some species even have multiple queens, making them almost impossible to eliminate with surface sprays alone.

⚠ Spraying scouts can split the colony & make it worse
🍳

Food Contamination

Ants walk through drains, rubbish and decaying matter before crawling across your food prep surfaces, pantry items and pet bowls. They carry bacteria with every step.

⚠ Salmonella & E. coli risk in kitchens
🏢

Structural Damage

Carpenter ants hollow out timber for nesting, while other species nest in wall cavities, power points and roof voids, causing electrical shorts and structural weakening.

⚠ Carpenter ants can rival termite damage

Our Ant Elimination Process

Melbourne's ant pest control experts target the queen. Kill the queen, kill the colony.

Species Identification

Our ant exterminators identify the exact ant species — black house ants, white-footed ants, coastal brown ants, or carpenter ants — because each requires a different treatment approach.

Gel Bait Application

Professional-grade gel baits placed along ant trails and entry points. Workers carry the bait back to the nest, feeding it to the queen and larvae — collapsing the entire colony.

External Perimeter Spray

Residual insecticide applied around the full perimeter of your home — foundations, weep holes, window frames and door thresholds — creating a chemical barrier ants cannot cross.

Internal Treatment

Targeted spray and dust treatment to internal hotspots such as behind kitchen cupboards, around plumbing, skirting boards and power points where ants nest and travel.

Nest Dusting

If nests are found in wall voids, roof spaces or garden beds, we apply insecticidal dust directly into the nest for rapid colony collapse.

Written Warranty

Every treatment comes with a written guarantee. If ants return during the warranty period, so do we — at no extra cost.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Ant Control

!

Ant Trails Along Walls

Lines of ants marching along skirting boards, window sills, benchtops or walls — especially heading towards food or water sources.

!

Ants in Pantry or Cupboards

Finding ants inside food packaging, around sugar, cereal or pet food. Even sealed containers may not stop determined ant colonies.

!

Small Dirt Mounds

Tiny piles of fine soil near cracks in pavers, along foundations or in garden beds indicate active nest entrances close to your home.

!

Ants After Rain

Sudden ant invasions after rainfall. Heavy rain floods underground nests, driving entire colonies inside your home seeking dry shelter and food.
