Why Treatment Matters

Mosquitoes Ruin More Than Barbecues

They carry diseases, breed in the tiniest amount of standing water, and turn your outdoor living areas into no-go zones.

🦟

Disease Carriers

Mosquitoes transmit Ross River virus, Barmah Forest virus, and dengue fever. Melbourne's warming climate is increasing mosquito-borne disease risk.

⚠ 5,000+ Ross River cases annually in Australia
💧

Rapid Breeders

A single female lays 100–300 eggs at a time in as little as a bottle cap of water. They breed in gutters, pot saucers, birdbaths, and blocked drains.

⚠ Egg to adult in just 7–10 days
🏡

Outdoor Living Destroyed

Mozzies are most active at dawn and dusk — exactly when you want to enjoy your deck, garden, or pool. They make outdoor living unbearable.

⚠ Citronella candles barely work

Our Mosquito Elimination Process

Property Inspection

Perimeter Protection

Signs You Need Mosquito Control

!

Can't Use the Backyard at Dusk

If mosquitoes swarm every time you step outside in the evening, you have an established population breeding on or near your property.

!

Standing Water on Property

Blocked gutters, birdbaths, pot saucers, old tyres, or any container with standing water is a mosquito breeding factory.

!

Bites on the Family

Waking up with bites or getting swarmed during outdoor activities means mosquitoes are well-established in your area and breeding nearby.

Near Wetlands or Creek

Properties near waterways, reserves, or wetlands experience significantly higher mosquito pressure and benefit most from barrier treatments.
