Why Treatment Matters

Rats Are Far More Dangerous Than You Think

They chew through wiring causing house fires, contaminate food with disease, and breed at an alarming rate. Without proper rat control, a small problem becomes a serious health and safety risk within weeks.

🔥

House Fire Risk

Rats gnaw through electrical wiring, gas lines and plumbing. Rodent-damaged wiring is a leading cause of house fires in Australia.

⚠ 25% of house fires from unknown causes are rodent-related
🐀

Disease Carriers

Rats spread leptospirosis, salmonella, hantavirus and parasites through droppings, urine and contaminated surfaces.

⚠ Rat urine can contaminate entire pantries
📈

Rapid Breeding

A single pair of rats can produce up to 1,500 offspring per year. A small problem becomes an infestation within weeks.

⚠ Rats reach sexual maturity in just 5 weeks

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Rat Control

!

Scratching Noises at Night

Scurrying, scratching or gnawing sounds in the roof, walls or under the floor — especially at night when rats are most active.

!

Droppings & Urine Stains

Dark, capsule-shaped droppings (12–18mm) found along walls, in cupboards, or near food storage areas.

!

Gnaw Marks & Damage

Chewed wiring, timber, plastic pipes, food packaging or insulation. Rats must gnaw constantly to keep their teeth short.

!

Grease Trails Along Walls

Rats leave dark, oily smear marks along walls and skirting boards from the grease in their fur as they follow the same paths nightly. If you spot these, it’s time to call a rat exterminator.
