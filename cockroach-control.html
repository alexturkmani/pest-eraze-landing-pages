Warning Signs

Signs You Have a Cockroach Problem

!

Pepper-Like Droppings

Small dark droppings in cupboards, drawers, and behind appliances are a telltale sign of cockroach activity.

!

Musty, Oily Smell

A strong, unpleasant musty or oily odour in the kitchen or bathroom often indicates a large hidden population.

!

Egg Casings (Oothecae)

Brown, capsule-shaped egg cases hidden in cracks mean active breeding. Each case contains 30–50 baby cockroaches.

!

Daytime Sightings

Cockroaches are nocturnal. If you see them during the day, the nest is overcrowded — meaning a serious infestation.
