Warning Signs

Signs You Have a Mouse Problem

!

Scratching in the Walls at Night

Hearing light scratching, rustling, or scurrying inside walls, ceilings, or under floors — especially at night — is the most common sign of mice activity.

!

Small Dark Droppings

Mouse droppings are 3–6mm long, dark, and pellet-shaped. You’ll find them in kitchen drawers, behind appliances, inside pantries, and along skirting boards.

!

Gnawed Food Packaging

Mice chew through cereal boxes, bread bags, and pet food containers. If you find small holes in stored food packaging, you have an active mouse problem.

!

Shredded Nesting Material

Finding shredded paper, fabric, insulation, or cardboard in hidden spots like behind the fridge, inside stored boxes, or in the roof void indicates active nesting.

!

Musty Ammonia Smell

A strong, stale ammonia odour — especially in enclosed spaces like cupboards or under sinks — indicates a well-established mouse colony.

!

Pets Acting Strangely

Cats or dogs pawing at walls, staring under the fridge, or becoming excited in certain areas of the house often signals mice activity behind surfaces.
