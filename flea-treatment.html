Why Treatment Matters

Fleas Aren't Just a Pet Problem

Fleas breed explosively, bite humans too, and can survive months without a host hidden deep in your carpets.

🦟

Explosive Lifecycle

One flea can lay 50 eggs per day. Eggs fall into carpets, cracks, and furniture where larvae develop unseen for weeks.

⚠ 95% of a flea population is hidden as eggs & larvae
😖

Itchy Bites on Everyone

Fleas don't just bite pets — they bite humans around ankles and legs. Bites cause intense itching, welts, and can trigger allergic reactions.

⚠ Flea allergy dermatitis is extremely common
🦠

Disease Carriers

Fleas transmit tapeworm, murine typhus, and bartonella (cat scratch disease). They're a genuine health hazard for families and pets.

⚠ Flea-bomb foggers push fleas deeper into hiding

Warning Signs

Signs You Have a Flea Infestation

!

Pet Scratching Excessively

Constant scratching, biting at fur, and restlessness in your dog or cat are the most obvious signs of flea activity.

!

Bites Around Ankles

Red, itchy bites clustered around ankles and lower legs when walking barefoot on carpet — a telltale flea sign.

!

Black Specks in Pet Fur

Tiny black specks (flea dirt/faeces) in your pet's coat. Place them on damp white paper — if they turn reddish, it's flea dirt.

!

Jumping Specks on Carpet

Seeing tiny dark specks jumping near carpet or furniture. White sock test: walk across carpet in white socks — fleas will jump onto them.
